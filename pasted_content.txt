> **🖥️ Website Structure**
>
> Build as a single-page scroll site with clear section anchors:
>
> 1. **Hero** — Powerful, professional headline. Vision-first. Evoke trust and curiosity. (e.g., "새로운 삶의 장을 설계합니다 / Designing the Next Chapter of Life")
> 2. **What We Do** — Two business pillars explained concisely. Concept-level only — no product listings or pricing.
> 3. **Who We Are** — Team & founding story. Credible and warm. Emphasize expertise and mission.
> 4. **Where We Go** — Vision roadmap. Milestone or timeline format. Forward-looking tone.
> 5. **How We Work** — Core values, work principles, partnership philosophy.
> 6. **Contact / Connect** — Clean contact form or email CTA. Separate CTAs for individuals vs B2B partners.
>
> ---
>
> **🎨 Design Direction**
>
> - **Tone & Mood:** Professional & corporate — clean, structured, trustworthy
> - **Feel:** "Established credibility" — like a well-funded company, not a garage startup
> - **Color Palette:** Deep forest green + white + dark navy or charcoal — natural yet authoritative
> - **Typography:** Structured, professional sans-serif. Korean/English bilingual-friendly (e.g., Noto Sans KR)
> - **Layout:** Grid-based, spacious, well-organized sections
> - **Imagery Style:** Professional lifestyle photography — active seniors, nature, clean workspace — NOT medical or elderly-care imagery
> - **Avoid:** Playful colors, casual fonts, cluttered layouts, e-commerce UI
>
> ---
>
> **📌 Key Design Principles**
>
> - **Vision-first**, not sales-first — we are pre-product stage
> - Must feel credible to **B2B partners and corporate clients**
> - Relatable and respectful tone toward **50s~60s audience** — dignified, not patronizing
> - Slightly **"gray area" narrative** — intriguing and open, not over-explained
> - No shopping cart, no product pricing
> - **Mobile responsive** is mandatory
> - All copy should be provided in **both Korean and English** side by side or toggleable
>
> ---
>
> **📄 Deliverables**
>
> - Full HTML/CSS/JS website (or React)
> - All placeholder text in Korean + English
> - Clean, well-commented code
> - Fully responsive (mobile + desktop + tablet)
> - Separate CTA styling for individual visitors vs B2B inquiries
